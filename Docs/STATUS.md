# Prototype status

First playable Windows build implemented on Unity 6000.3.26f1.

Delivered: adventure map and route arrows, day/movement economy, joining encounter, blocking encounter, hex combat with simple enemy AI, town recruitment, weekly stock growth, ownership through combat, protected clan home army, owned home/visiting army transfer, hero home/visiting placement, gold/XP/reward artifacts, equipment/inventory, level bonuses, victory/defeat/retry, save/load, sound-state layers, distinct hits and creature calls, audio options and F3 audition/development controls.

Verified by 30 Editor rules checks, Windows player build, full campaign simulation including movement/encounter boundaries, town conquest and both hero positions, and screenshots inspected for map, tactical battle, hero, neutral/owned town, victory and defeat. Latest generated evidence is in Reports/ and build.log (not committed).

The visuals and synthetic audio are placeholders. Recorded guitar stems and final voice performances remain a creative production milestone. The prototype does not include town construction/investment, negotiations, spells, multiple heroes, partial-stack splitting, multiplayer or campaigns beyond this mission.

Next useful iteration: user playthrough and listening feedback, especially gallop timing, perceived danger, impact intensity, combat pacing and recruitment usability. Then replace placeholder audio and art while preserving the working mission loop.
