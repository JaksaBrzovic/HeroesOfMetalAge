# First mission: The Ashen Pass

One playable hero, Kael Ironsong, starts on day 1 with 1,600 gold and 12 Roadies at the western end of the road. The adventure map is the painted expanded Ashen Pass, 100 x 61 square cells: two thirds green forest roads and clearings in the west, the mountain ridge with its pass, the castle of Ashen Hold and one third ash land in the east. 1,600 movement points renew each day; a cell costs 75 on the road, 100 on grass or dirt, 125 on rough ash ground, 150 on sand and 175 in swamp, and a diagonal step 1.41 times that. Trees, mountains, water, lava and obstacles block a mounted hero. Diagonal movement cannot cut blocked corners. Encounters and the objects that are taken or opened stop traversal and require interaction; lasting places (shrines, the mill, the camp, the skald, the oracle, the portal) are ridden over and visited when they are the destination.

Armies: the Willing (6 Air Guitarists) and a road crew (8 Roadies) join for free. Forest bandits (9 Roadies, 4 Air Guitarists) guard the south; the Iron Reavers (3 Berserkers, 10 Roadies) sit on the Gloves of Metal; the pass wardens (14 Roadies, 6 Air Guitarists) hold the way to Ashen Hold; ash revenants (5 Berserkers, 10 Air Guitarists) roam the ash land; a minotaur pack (6 Minotaurs) guards the chained maiden; the Black Banner (24 Roadies, 14 Air Guitarists, 6 Berserkers) guards the Gate of Fire.

Map objects: three gold piles (400, 600, 800), an old chest (1,000 gold or 500 XP), two artefacts on the ground, the Bloody Colosseum (+2 attack), the Iron Bastion (+2 defense), the Shrine of the Horns (+2 morale until the next victory), the old watermill (750 gold a week), the Roadie camp (8 Roadies a week at full price), the old skald (the red key), the red tent (opens for the red key), the chained maiden (Chain of the Iron Maiden, epic), the bone oracle (the sleeping dragon) and the Gate of Fire (the exit).

Goals: defeat the Black Banner (required), free the chained maiden, find the Gloves of Metal, learn of the sleeping dragon, reach level 5. The Gate of Fire offers to finish the mission once the required goal is reached and shows how many goals were reached; finishing returns to the main menu.

Creature army slots: seven; equal types merge. Initial town stock/growth is 14 Roadies, 9 Air Guitarists and 4 Berserkers. Stocks accumulate rather than reset. Prices: 70, 110 and 260 gold. Clan territory protects its home army (16 Roadies, 8 Air Guitarists). Ownership may be gained by fighting this army. Ownership enables stack transfer and production prices at 55% of neutral prices, rounded up.

Hero starts with attack 2 and defense 2. Every 300 XP grants a level; attack increases each level, defense every two levels. Eleven equipment places: helm, left arm (shield), right arm (weapon), gloves, torso, two rings, amulet, necklace, sash and boots. Twenty-eight artefacts from common to legendary add attack, defense, speed or morale. Morale -3 to +3 (artefacts, the shrine): each point is a 10% chance that a stack which moved or struck acts again (good morale) or that a stack freezes (bad morale).

Combat: 10 x 7 odd-row hex grid. One action per stack per round (good morale may give a second). Speed determines initiative. Actions: move, attack (including move-and-melee), defend, pass. One retaliation per round. Casualties persist after victory. Top surviving creatures are fully healed between battles. Defeat does not persist casualties and offers retry/preparation/new mission.

Acceptance: reach every site, recruit at the town and the camp, equip rewards, free the maiden with the key, defeat the Black Banner and leave through the Gate of Fire; observe independent victory/defeat screens, weekly growth, protected neutral home slots and transferable owned slots, and audible context changes. Compilation alone is insufficient: run the EditMode tests and the built-player smoke simulation.
