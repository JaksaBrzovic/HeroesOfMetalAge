# First mission: The Ashen Pass

One playable hero, Kael Ironsong, starts on day 1 with 1,600 gold and 12 Roadies at the western end of the road. The adventure map is the painted Ashen Pass, 50 x 30 square cells. 1,600 movement points renew each day; a cell costs 75 on the road, 100 on grass or dirt, 125 on rough ash ground, 150 on sand and 175 in swamp, and a diagonal step 1.41 times that. Trees, mountains, water, lava and obstacles block a mounted hero. Diagonal movement cannot cut blocked corners. Encounters stop traversal and require interaction.

Six Air Guitarists in a clearing beside the road offer to join for free. Ten Roadies guard the only mountain pass. Their defeat grants 180 XP, 500 gold and the Fang of Thunder (+2 attack). Town recruitment is accessible beyond the pass. The Black Banner waits in the eastern clearing with 18 Roadies, 9 Air Guitarists and 3 Doom Riders, and grants 450 XP, 900 gold and the Warborn Sigil (+1 speed).

Creature army slots: seven; equal types merge. Initial town stock/growth is 14 Roadies, 9 Air Guitarists and 4 Doom Riders. Stocks accumulate rather than reset. Prices: 70, 110 and 260 gold. Clan territory protects its home army (13 Roadies, 5 Air Guitarists). Ownership may be gained by fighting this army. Ownership enables stack transfer and production prices at 55% of neutral prices, rounded up.

Hero starts with attack 2 and defense 2. Every 300 XP grants a level; attack increases each level, defense every two levels. Equipment slots: weapon and charm. Fang of Thunder adds attack 2; Ashen Ward adds defense 2; Warborn Sigil adds speed 1. Ashen Ward is available through the development panel for testing; normal mission rewards the other two.

Combat: 10 x 7 odd-row hex grid. One action per stack per round. Speed determines initiative. Actions: move, attack (including move-and-melee), defend, pass. One retaliation per round. Casualties persist after victory. Top surviving creatures are fully healed between battles. Defeat does not persist casualties and offers retry/preparation/new mission.

Acceptance: complete all four map events, recruit at town, equip reward, defeat final force, observe independent victory/defeat screens, weekly growth, protected neutral home slots and transferable owned slots, and audible context changes. Compilation alone is insufficient: run the EditMode tests and the built-player smoke simulation.
