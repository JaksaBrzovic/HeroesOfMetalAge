# First mission: The Ashen Pass

One playable hero, Kael Ironsong, starts on day 1 with 1,600 gold and 12 Ironbound. The adventure map is 18 x 11 square cells. Road/open ground costs one movement point; forest costs two. Sixteen movement points renew each day. Mountains and water block travel. Diagonal movement cannot cut blocked corners. Encounters stop traversal and require interaction.

Six Ash Archers offer to join for free. Ten Ironbound guard the only pass. Their defeat grants 180 XP, 500 gold and the Fang of Thunder (+2 attack). Town recruitment is accessible beyond the pass. The Black Banner contains 18 Ironbound, 9 Ash Archers and 3 Doom Riders, and grants 450 XP, 900 gold and the Warborn Sigil (+1 speed).

Creature army slots: seven; equal types merge. Initial town stock/growth is 14 Ironbound, 9 Ash Archers and 4 Doom Riders. Stocks accumulate rather than reset. Prices: 70, 110 and 260 gold. Clan territory protects its home army (13 Ironbound, 5 Ash Archers). Ownership may be gained by fighting this army. Ownership enables stack transfer and production prices at 55% of neutral prices, rounded up.

Hero starts with attack 2 and defense 2. Every 300 XP grants a level; attack increases each level, defense every two levels. Equipment slots: weapon and charm. Fang of Thunder adds attack 2; Ashen Ward adds defense 2; Warborn Sigil adds speed 1. Ashen Ward is available through the development panel for testing; normal mission rewards the other two.

Combat: 10 x 7 odd-row hex grid. One action per stack per round. Speed determines initiative. Actions: move, attack (including move-and-melee), defend, pass. One retaliation per round. Casualties persist after victory. Top surviving creatures are fully healed between battles. Defeat does not persist casualties and offers retry/preparation/new mission.

Acceptance: complete all four map events, recruit at town, equip reward, defeat final force, observe independent victory/defeat screens, weekly growth, protected neutral home slots and transferable owned slots, and audible context changes. Compilation alone is insufficient: run rules checks and built-player smoke simulation.
